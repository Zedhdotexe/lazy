PHASE #1

After 33% of tickets are sold, the first phase will commence, and a video will be posted on all our social media accounts with the lucky number! All tickets that do not have the first number, are now losing tickets, but still remain as an OGpass for life!

PHASE #2

After 66% of the tickets have sold, the second phase will commence with a second video posting the next lucky number.


All holders of possible grand prize winning tickets will check into the circle chat in the discord group. There we will celebrate, discuss together ideas on how much they want their floor price to be for resale, what future prizes we all think would be amazing! AND MORE!

PHASE #3

The final and third phase will commence and a winner will be chosen after 100% of tickets are sold. If there is a peak of ticket sales above 90%, then the final number will be picked anyways. All remaining tickets will be left on the market for grabs as OG passes. Enjoy the fun everyone!
